System Safety

Honest Guardrails: Ricing Without Regret

Your system is your workshop. Buckwheat respects your dotfiles and never blows away your custom configuration.

01 / OVERLAYS

Managed Overlays, Not Overwrites

Buckwheat writes exclusively to dedicated include files (e.g. ~/.config/hypr/buckwheat.conf). Your master config files remain completely untouched and clean.

02 / TRANSPARENCY

Inspect Before You Apply

Every single config change is rendered in a clean, color-coded unified diff preview. You always have full visibility into what will be written before anything touches disk.

03 / TIMED TIMEOUT

Timed Revert Safety Net

When applying a new theme, a 30-second watchdog timer starts. If the new setup breaks readability or you dislike it, press Esc or wait—it automatically restores your previous state.

04 / SCHEMAS

Structured Changes

Buckwheat generates targeted, schema-validated AST adjustments (via Pydantic ChangePlan models)—never opaque, uncontrolled shell scripts.
